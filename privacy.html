Dating Rizz Privacy Policy

Effective November 15, 2026

The short version
  • No account. We never ask for your name, email, phone number, or birthday.
  • The app is free and shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may use your device's advertising ID and basic device information to show and measure those ads.
  • Your saves, votes, and settings stay on your phone. We don't run any servers and we don't collect anything ourselves.
  • You can remove ads with a one-time purchase. Payments are handled entirely by Apple or Google, and we never see your card details.

Who we are

Dating Rizz ("the app", "we", "us") is a mobile app that shows a feed of pickup lines you can browse, vote on, save, and share, plus a glossary of Gen Z slang. This policy explains what information the app handles and what happens to it.

Information we collect

We don't collect any personal information ourselves. We do not operate any servers for the app, and nothing you do in the app is sent to us. The only information that leaves your device is what Google's advertising service collects to show ads, described below.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

To keep Dating Rizz free, the app shows ads provided by Google AdMob. When an ad is requested, Google's software in the app may collect:

  • Your device's advertising ID (Android advertising ID or Apple IDFA, if you allow it)
  • Your IP address, which can indicate your approximate location (city or region)
  • Device information, such as model, operating system, language, and screen size
  • How you interact with ads, such as views and taps

Google uses this information to show ads, measure how they perform, limit how often you see the same ad, and prevent fraud. Depending on your choices and where you live, ads may be personalized based on your interests. Google handles this information under its own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services

We limit ads in the app to content rated suitable for general audiences with parental guidance (PG).

Your ad choices

  • Europe and the UK: when you first open the app, you'll see Google's consent form, where you can choose whether to allow personalized ads.
  • iPhone: the app asks for permission before your advertising ID can be used for tracking. You can change this any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalization, in your phone's Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • No ads at all: buy "Remove ads" in the app. Once ads are removed, the app no longer loads Google's advertising service.

Information stored on your device

To work, the app keeps a small amount of data locally on your phone, using your device's standard app storage:

  • Lines you save, like, or vote on (W or L)
  • Whether you have bought "Remove ads"

This data never leaves your device and is not sent to us. It is deleted when you delete the app. It may be included in your own device backups (for example iCloud or Google backups), which are governed by Apple's or Google's privacy policies.

Purchases

Dating Rizz offers a one-time "Remove ads" purchase. All payments are processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play. They handle your payment details, billing, and purchase history under their own privacy policies. We do not receive your name, email, or payment information.

To check whether you own "Remove ads", the app asks the App Store or Google Play directly on your device. That check happens between your phone and Apple or Google; the results are not sent to us.

  • Apple Privacy Policy
  • Google Privacy Policy

Sharing lines

When you tap Share, the app creates the image or text on your device and opens your phone's standard share sheet. The content goes only where you choose to send it, and we do not see it.

Children

Dating Rizz is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. If you believe a child under 13 is using the app, you can delete it from their device, which removes all of its data.

Your rights

Because we do not collect or store personal information ourselves, there is nothing for us to access, correct, export, or delete on your behalf. You can remove all app data at any time by deleting the app or clearing its storage in your device settings. For information Google collects to show ads, you can use the choices above or Google's tools at My Ad Center.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change, we will update this page and change the effective date above before the change takes effect.
