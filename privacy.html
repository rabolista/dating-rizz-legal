Dating Rizz Privacy Policy

Effective October 13, 2026

The short version
  • No account. We never ask for your name, email, phone number, or birthday.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking.
  • Your saves, votes, and settings stay on your phone.
  • Payments are handled entirely by Apple or Google. We never see your card details.

Who we are

Dating Rizz ("the app", "we", "us") is a mobile app that shows a feed of pickup lines you can browse, vote on, save, and share. This policy explains what information the app handles and what happens to it.

Information we collect

None. We do not operate any servers for the app, and the app does not send any personal information to us or to any third party for analytics, advertising, or tracking. We cannot see who uses the app or what they do in it.

Information stored on your device

To work, the app keeps a small amount of data locally on your phone, using your device's standard app storage:

  • Lines you save, like, or vote on (W or L)
  • How many free lines you have viewed today, so the daily free limit works
  • Whether you have unlocked unlimited access

This data never leaves your device. It is deleted when you delete the app. It may be included in your own device backups (for example iCloud or Google backups), which are governed by Apple's or Google's privacy policies.

Purchases and subscriptions

Dating Rizz offers a one-time "Unlock forever" purchase and an optional monthly subscription. All payments are processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play. They handle your payment details, billing, and purchase history under their own privacy policies. We do not receive your name, email, or payment information.

To check whether you have an active purchase, the app asks the App Store or Google Play directly on your device. That check happens between your phone and Apple or Google; the results are not sent to us.

  • Apple Privacy Policy
  • Google Privacy Policy

Sharing lines

When you tap Share, the app creates the image or text on your device and opens your phone's standard share sheet. The content goes only where you choose to send it, and we do not see it.

Children

Dating Rizz is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Because the app does not collect personal information at all, there is no personal information about children to access or delete.

Your choices and rights

Because we do not collect or store personal information, there is nothing for us to access, correct, export, or delete on your behalf. You can remove all app data at any time by deleting the app or clearing its storage in your device settings. To manage or cancel a subscription, use your App Store or Google Play account settings.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change, we will update this page and change the effective date above before the change takes effect.

Contact
